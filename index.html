Run a pre-submission self-check on your permit package before you file it. Nothing uploads anywhere. Everything runs in your browser.

What this is not. This is a pre-submission self-check, not a permit approval, not a code compliance review, and not a substitute for the county's plan review or your design professional's seal. Requirements change. Always confirm against the county's own pages before you submit.
