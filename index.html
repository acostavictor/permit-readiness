        How to use Permit Validate

        Three steps, about two minutes.

        1. Pick where it is going

        Choose the county or city that issues the permit. City permitting is separate from county permitting, so a property inside Orlando city limits goes to the City of Orlando, not Orange County Fast Track. The same applies in every city listed.

        2. Drop your package

        PDFs only: the drawing set plus supporting documents like the site plan, energy forms, product approvals, and truss drawings. Analysis starts on its own the moment you drop them. Nothing uploads anywhere. Everything runs in your browser. Click the X on a file to remove it and re-run the check.

        3. Read the results

        Missing shows what the app could not find. Found shows what it did find, file by file. Verify by hand lists inconsistencies and anything software cannot confirm. Below that, your checklist fills itself in from the package: items marked "Found in your package" were auto-checked. Confirm each one yourself, tick the rest by hand, then close the gaps before you submit. Use Print this report for a copy to work from.

        Two things to know

        • If a PDF is a scan or has no text layer, the app says "Could not verify" instead of "missing." That means it could not read the file, not that the item is absent. A human must check those sheets.
        • The jurisdiction makes the final call. This is a pre-submission self-check, not a permit approval and not a code compliance review. When in doubt, check the jurisdiction's own pages, linked under Sources at the bottom of the page.
