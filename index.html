Permit Readiness Check
Osceola and Orange County, FL

Run a pre-submission self-check on your permit package before you file it. Upload the package and the app checks it, then lists what a human still has to verify. Nothing uploads anywhere. Everything runs in your browser.

What this is not. This is a pre-submission self-check, not a permit approval, not a code compliance review, and not a substitute for the county's plan review or your design professional's seal. Automated findings are heuristic: keyword and text signals from your PDFs, not proof. Every flagged item needs a human to confirm it. Requirements change. Always confirm against the county's own pages before you submit.

1. Where is the project?

Pick the county that issues the permit. If the property is inside Orlando city limits, that is a different office (City of Orlando permitting), not Orange County Fast Track.

2. Check your package

Tick each item you have ready. Items marked COUNTY come from the county's own published pages. Items marked DRAWING are drawing-quality checks reused from Markup & Validate.

3. Upload your permit package (PDFs only)

Drop in the drawing set and the supporting documents: site plan, energy forms, product approvals, truss drawings. The app reads the text of each PDF in your browser and checks it against the county list. If a PDF is a scan with no extractable text, the text checks cannot run on it and it goes on the human list.
