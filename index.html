ready for the county?

Drop in your package. The app checks it against the county's requirements, tells you what is missing, and builds your checklist from what it found. Nothing uploads anywhere. Everything runs in your browser.

What this is not. This is a pre-submission self-check, not a permit approval, not a code compliance review, and not a substitute for the county's plan review or your design professional's seal. Automated findings are heuristic: keyword and text signals from your PDFs, not proof. Every flagged item needs a human to confirm it. Requirements change. Always confirm against the county's own pages before you submit.

1. Where is it going?
